2 Disposal vs. Pending Fetch Logic (Forms 1 to 5 & Form 6) Data Automation Logic
Counter Field Trigger & Fetch Condition Qualified Disposal Fields (No & Date) Default Action
DISPOSAL
Disposed		 A case in Form 1 to 5 and Form 6 is fetched into DISPOSAL when any one of the 7 official finalization Number & Date pairs is filled:
  • CC. No & Date
  • ST. No & Date
  • A Final No & Date
  • B Final No & Date
  • C Final No & Date
  • NC Final No & Date
  • Abated Summary No & Date
 Moved to Disposal tab & counted in sub-tab disposal metric.
PENDING
Pending		 When none of the 7 disposal numbers & dates are filled (all remain blank/empty), the case automatically stays/fetches into PENDING. All 7 disposal fields are empty/null (Active IO investigation, awaiting charge sheet, or trial pending). Remains in Pending tab requiring police investigation attention.
TOTAL
All Cases		 TOTAL = PENDING + DISPOSAL (Sum of all registered cases in the sub-tab). Resets table filter to show all records.
3 Sub-Tab Page Feature Matrix Operational Capabilities
Feature Component Description User Benefit
Header Summary Cards Three live metric cards: Total, Pending, and Disposal with sub-text indicators. Gives instant supervisory overview of crime status in that category.
One-Click Metric Filtering Clicking any counter card instantly filters the case data table below without reloading the page. Enables fast triage of pending workload for officers and reviewers.
Search & Date Range Filter Search by CR No, Accused Name, Complainant Name, IO Name, and filter by Registration Date range. Quickly locates specific cases during scrutiny or inspection.
Category-Aware `+ New Case` Opens the Common Form with the sub-tab's crime category and core IPC/BNS Section pre-selected. Eliminates repetitive data entry and minimizes misclassification errors.
Row Action Suite Direct buttons for View Detail, Edit Form, Generate Case PDF, and Transfer. Streamlined access to complete case actions directly from the sub-tab table.
